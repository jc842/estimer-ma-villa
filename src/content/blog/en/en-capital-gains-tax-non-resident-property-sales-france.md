# Capital Gains Tax for Non-Resident Real Estate Sales in French Territories

Navigating capital gains tax (*plus-value immobilière*) is essential for optimizing net divestment proceeds.

---

## 1. Sliding Scale Taper Relief (*Abattement pour Durée de Détention*)
- Total income tax exemption on real estate capital gains after 22 years of ownership.
- Total social charge (*prélèvements sociaux*) exemption after 30 years of continuous ownership.

## 2. Deductible Renovation Work
Major structural improvements, expansions, and certified builder invoices reduce taxable capital gains, provided work was performed by licensed contractors.
