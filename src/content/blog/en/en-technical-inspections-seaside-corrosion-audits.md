# Coastal Technical Audits: Diagnosing Salt Mist Corrosion & Structural Soundness

Tropical maritime environments accelerate weathering on structural concrete and electro-mechanical installations.

---

## 1. Concrete Spalling and Carbonation
Airborne marine chlorides penetrate porous concrete over decades, oxidizing internal steel rebar and causing expansive cracking if not properly waterproofed.

## 2. Roof Anchor Systems & Cyclone Readiness
Certified appraisals verify heavy-gauge tie-down hurricane strapping connecting timber roof trusses directly into the reinforced concrete bond beam ring.
