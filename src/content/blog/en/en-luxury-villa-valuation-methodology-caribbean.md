# Luxury Villa Valuation: Comprehensive Appraisal Methodology for Seaside Estates

Valuing luxury Caribbean residential properties requires far more than basic square-meter averages used in standard metropolitan developments.

---

## 1. The Value Multipliers of Luxury Waterfront Estates
- **Unobstructed Sea View Premium**: Adds between 25% and 50% to land parcel valuations compared to equivalent inland parcels.
- **Direct Beach or Private Moorings Access**: Direct private boat dockage in sheltered bays commands significant off-market premiums.
- **Architectural Pedigree & Finishes**: Structural exotic timber (*iroko*, *teak*), infinity-edge pools, and custom hurricane-rated pocket joinery.

## 2. Quantitative vs. Comparative Appraisals
Combining recent notary land registry transaction databases (*DVF*) with discounted rental cash-flow yields determines an accurate fair market valuation (*valeur vénale*).
